- `oxa lead board` shows and edits the lead kanban from a terminal: add a card or a planning chunk from JSON, edit, move, reorder, split, stick, unstick, note, link a PR, remove and undo through the typed operations of the kanban store, set per-host capacity and the goal with its measures, and turn dispatch on or off. `--json` prints the typed board, an item, a lane, the goal, the capacity map or the applied sequence number, and a card printed by `show --json` is accepted again by `add --from-json`. The actor comes from the environment: a terminal is the owner, a lead session is the lead, and a worker with `OXA_BOARD_ITEM` may only note or stick its own card (#2373).
