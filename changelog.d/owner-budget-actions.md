- Owner caps are user-only and the owner chooses what exceeding one does. New user-only `oxa.onExceed` sets `warn`, `pause` or `stop` per budget (`bashJobs`, `subagentChildren`, `goalIdle`, `goalUnknownUsage`). Background jobs and subagent children still warn by default, now with a recorded notice, and refuse over the cap with `stop` (#1964, #1967). A goal with no other budget pauses after two replies without a tool call, and a goal under a token cap pauses when a request reports no usage (#1966, #1963). `oxa.tools.wait`, `oxa.bash.maxJobs`, `oxa.subagent.maxChildren` and `oxa.toolOutputCap` can no longer be set from a project file (#1918, #1965).
