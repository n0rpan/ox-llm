- `worker start` takes `kind`, `job` and `reason`; the user-only `oxa.workers.models` setting (default and per-kind model lists, `escalateAfter`, `escalateTo`) chooses the worker model, the choice and why are recorded and shown by `worker list`, and the new `worker result` action records a failed attempt so the next one on the same job escalates. An unknown model id warns and still starts. With no setting nothing changes.
