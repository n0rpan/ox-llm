usage: export_receipt.py [-h] {export,scan,rules} ...

Turn private run receipts into PUBLIC-SAFE receipts, and scan files for
private data.

positional arguments:
  {export,scan,rules}
    export             private receipts -> one public-safe receipt
    scan               scan files or directories; exit 1 on hits
    rules              list scan rules

options:
  -h, --help           show this help message and exit
