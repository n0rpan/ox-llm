Usage:
  oxc share [status] [--json]      what is shared, where, and the key fingerprint
  oxc share on [tailscale|lan]     share the OpenAI API with your devices (default: tailscale)
  oxc share off                    loopback only again, at once
  oxc share key [--rotate]         show the API key once; --rotate makes a new one (old stops working)
